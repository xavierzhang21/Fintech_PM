ScotiaConnect Digital Channels

Product owner for Scotiabank's ScotiaConnect digital channels, with full lifecycle accountability across payments, deposits, statements, analytics, and authentication for more than 60,000 business banking customers.

Organization
Scotiabank, Global Transaction Banking
Role
Product Manager, Digital Channels
Period
Apr 2022 — May 2024
Impact
6% payments revenue · $12M CIAM funding · ~$800K cost savings

Context

ScotiaConnect is Scotiabank's flagship digital banking platform for business customers. As Product Manager for Digital Channels, I owned the full product lifecycle across payments, deposits, statements, analytics, and authentication.

6% EFT/Wire revenue increase · Wire tracking success 5%→80% · ~$800K estimated cost reductions from Online Loan Statements · $12M annual CIAM funding secured · GIC conversion doubled

EFT & Wire Payments

  • Managed full lifecycle of EFT and Wire payments products, leading a 10-person cross-functional agile team.
  • Released new payment flows to the full customer base, increasing payments revenue by 6%.

Online Loan Statements

  • Led the release of the Online Loan Statement product — the #1 NPS detractor for years.
  • Cut ~500 manual hours and 400 helpdesk calls per month, generating an estimated $800K from cost reductions.

GIC Online Purchase

  • Led design and go-to-market strategy for GIC online purchase capabilities for the small business segment.
  • Doubled purchase flow conversion rate through journey optimization and digital sales enablement.

Adobe Analytics & Wire Tracking

  • Introduced Adobe Analytics to ScotiaConnect as the first product owner to do so.
  • Presented insights to senior leadership, secured funding for iteration two, and lifted incoming wire tracking success rate from 5% to 80%.

CIAM Strategy

Audited authentication flows across ScotiaConnect, designed the future-state CIAM solution, and secured $12M in annual funding approval after presenting to leadership.

Full product specifications, Adobe Analytics dashboards, and CIAM strategy documentation available upon request under NDA.
